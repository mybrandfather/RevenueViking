CONTENT RULES
-------------
- Write for a real business-owner question, not a keyword count.
- Use cautious language around revenue, conversion and missed-call behavior.
- Do not invent industry statistics without a credible source.
- Do not promise automatic booking, CRM, SMS, dispatch or other integrations unless the article clearly labels them as optional workflows.
- Every indexed article should have a distinct title, description, H1 and body.
- Link to the relevant industry page, calculator/demo and one or two genuinely related articles.
- Prefer updating the 9 strong articles before creating more content.
