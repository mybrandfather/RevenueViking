PILLAR PAGE ARCHITECTURE
------------------------
Each pillar is a comprehensive 2,500+ word guide. Cluster articles
link UP to their pillar; the pillar links DOWN to every cluster
article. Industry pages link to relevant pillars and clusters.

PILLAR 1: The Complete Guide to AI Receptionists for Home Services
  → links to all "AI Receptionists" cluster articles
PILLAR 2: HVAC Marketing: The Definitive Growth Guide
  → links to all "HVAC Marketing" cluster articles
PILLAR 3: Local SEO for Service Businesses: Rank, Get Called, Get Hired
  → links to all "Local SEO" cluster articles
PILLAR 4: Lead Generation for the Trades: From First Ring to Booked Job
  → links to all "Lead Generation" + "Appointment Booking" articles

INTERNAL LINKING RULES
----------------------
1. Every cluster article links to its pillar in the intro or first H2.
2. Every article links to 2–3 sibling articles in the same category.
3. Every article links to ONE industry page (/ai-receptionist-for-*).
4. Every article ends with a CTA block linking to /book-demo.html.
5. Pillars link to /pricing.html once and the calculator section once.
