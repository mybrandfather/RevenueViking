Questions

Founding Client FAQ

What is an AI receptionist?

It is a voice-based call-intake system configured to use your approved greeting, questions and business information. RevenueViking’s standard offer focuses on answering routed calls and capturing structured customer details for follow-up.

Will it replace my existing phone number?

Not necessarily. The practical phone-routing setup depends on your current provider and when you want calls sent to the receptionist. We confirm that setup before launch.

What information can it collect?

Depending on the agreed workflow, it can collect the caller’s name, callback number, service address, reason for calling, problem details, urgency indicators and other approved business-specific information.

What happens after I apply?

We review your business and contact you about a relevant founding-client demo. You will not be asked for payment information on this application page.

Why is the founding price lower?

We are building a small founding customer group and want close feedback from real home-service businesses. A testimonial or case study may be invited if you are happy, but it is not mandatory.

Are call and usage costs included?

No unlimited usage is included or promised. Usage and infrastructure costs may apply based on actual call volume, providers and the configuration agreed with you.

What happens after the first year?

Post-year-one pricing has not been finalized. We will confirm the available pricing and terms with you before renewal rather than inventing or hiding a future rate.
