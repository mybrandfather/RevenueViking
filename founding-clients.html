Questions

Founding Client FAQ

Does Rachel replace my business phone number?

Usually no. Rachel can be connected through call forwarding or a routing setup that is agreed before launch. The right setup depends on your current phone provider and when you want calls answered.

What happens during emergencies?

Rachel can collect urgent details such as the problem, service address, callback number, and approved urgency indicators. Emergency handling rules are reviewed during setup so the receptionist does not promise service your team has not approved.

How do I get the lead details?

The delivery method is confirmed during setup. The founding offer focuses on making sure your team receives a clear lead summary with the caller information and service request.

Can I review calls?

Call review options depend on the provider configuration and consent requirements. We review what is available and appropriate for your business before launch.

What does setup cost?

Founding clients pay $200 one time for setup. The first 3 months are $97/month, then months 4-12 are $197/month. Regular pricing is $500 setup plus $297/month.

Can I cancel?

The application page does not lock you into payment. Any cancellation terms are reviewed before you become a paying client so you know what you are agreeing to.

Is this for small businesses?

Yes. RevenueViking is built for small contractors and local service companies where the owner, office manager, or field team cannot always answer every call.

Are call and usage costs included?

No unlimited usage is included or promised. Usage and infrastructure costs may apply based on actual call volume, providers, and the configuration agreed with you.
