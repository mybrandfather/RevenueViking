Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us — including your name, email address, phone number, business name, and business website — when you fill out a contact form, book a demo, or sign up for a plan. We also collect information about your use of our service, including call logs, lead data captured through the AI system, and analytics data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide and improve our service, process transactions, send service communications (including call summaries and booking notifications), respond to your enquiries, and to comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Call Recording and AI Processing

Our AI service processes calls in real time. Call content may be processed by our AI systems to generate summaries and lead information. By using our service, you agree to ensure that your customers are notified of call recording/AI processing in compliance with applicable laws in your jurisdiction.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Call summaries and lead data are retained for 24 months by default. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@revenueviking.com.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Contact Us

For privacy-related enquiries, email us at privacy@revenueviking.com or write to RevenueViking AI, Privacy Team, USA.
