# Hero Product Visual — Design QA

## Scope

Compared the approved homepage hero implementation with the supplied visual reference. The reference was used only for the three-stage product-story concept; the live RevenueViking design system, copy, technician video, claims policy, pricing, forms, and page structure remain authoritative.

## Comparison findings

- The implementation preserves the existing dark/gold RevenueViking look and HVAC technician footage.
- The product story is now visible in the hero as: Incoming Call → AI Receptionist Answers → Lead Captured.
- The new panel stays below the technician's face and phone area on desktop.
- On tablet and mobile, the video remains a separate supporting visual and the product steps move beneath it without horizontal overflow.
- The primary CTA remains Book a Free Demo. The secondary CTA now points to the existing How It Works section.
- No messaging shortcut, phone number, testimonials, reviews, customer logos, trust badges, statistics, guarantees, or unsupported claims were introduced.
