Pricing Questions

Is there a free trial?
+
We don't offer a self-serve free trial — instead, we offer a free live demo where you see the AI answer a real call for your business. After that, all paid plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee, so there's zero risk to getting started.
Can I change plans later?
+
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What's included in the setup fee?
+
The one-time setup fee covers: AI configuration and training for your specific business, custom greeting and script writing, calendar and CRM integration, call forwarding setup, full end-to-end testing, and a go-live walkthrough with your account manager.
Are there any hidden fees?
+
No. The setup fee and monthly fee are the only costs. There are no per-call fees on Growth and Pro plans. Starter is limited to 300 calls per month — if you exceed that, we'll contact you about upgrading before charging anything extra.
