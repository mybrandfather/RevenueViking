FAQ

Straight Questions.
Straight Answers.

What RevenueViking does today, what is optional, and what we will not promise before it is scoped and tested.

What does RevenueViking do today?
+
RevenueViking configures an AI receptionist around your business. A typical setup can greet callers, ask approved questions, collect name, callback number, service address, requested service and urgency information, then produce structured lead details for follow-up.
Can it handle emergency or urgent calls?
+
It can be configured to listen for the emergency indicators you define and use approved escalation language. The AI is not a substitute for 911, emergency services, a dispatcher or a qualified technician.
Will it work with my existing phone number?
+
In many setups, existing business calls can be routed or forwarded to the AI receptionist. The exact configuration depends on your phone carrier and desired call flow, so we verify it during setup.
Does the base package include appointment booking?
+
No. Appointment booking is a separate workflow. If you want it, we first review your calendar, job durations, service area, buffers, technician availability and exceptions. It is only promised after the workflow is scoped and tested.
Does it include CRM or SMS integration?
+
Not by default. CRM, SMS, calendar and other integrations can be quoted separately after we confirm the provider, access, fields, consent requirements, costs and error handling.
How long does setup take?
+
Setup time depends on your call flow, FAQs, phone routing, emergency rules and testing. We provide a realistic timeline after reviewing your requirements rather than using the same promise for every business.
Is AI usage unlimited?
+
No. Voice AI has real usage and provider costs. The founding-client price is $297 per month plus agreed usage/infrastructure costs, so there is no misleading unlimited-usage promise.
Can the AI replace my office staff?
+
The strongest initial use case is overflow and after-hours intake. A good human team remains better for many judgment calls, customer relationships, dispatch decisions and exceptions.
Does the AI give prices or diagnose problems?
+
Only information you explicitly approve should be used. We do not recommend letting the agent invent prices, diagnose technical problems, promise arrival times or give unsafe repair instructions.
How do I request a demo?
+
Use the demo form or email hello@revenueviking.com. Tell us your trade and the type of calls you receive, and we can show a relevant example.
