Our Story

We've Been On That Job Site

RevenueViking AI was founded in 2024 by a team with deep roots in the trades and in AI technology. Our founders watched family members and friends run HVAC and plumbing businesses — brilliant at their craft, but losing thousands every month because no one answered the phone when they were on a job.

The solution wasn't another answering service staffed by people who didn't understand the business. It was AI — trained specifically on trade and home service businesses — that could sound professional, answer the right questions, and book the job automatically.

Today, RevenueViking AI is built for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, and home service businesses across the United States. Every feature we build comes directly from feedback from real contractors in the field.
