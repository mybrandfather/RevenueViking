About RevenueViking

A Small, Focused Service
Built Around a Real Business Problem.

RevenueViking is being built to help local service businesses handle overflow and after-hours call intake more consistently — without pretending AI can replace every human decision.

Why This Exists

When the Team Is Busy, the Caller Still Needs a Path Forward

Home-service companies often depend on phone calls for new work, but owners and technicians spend much of the day away from a desk. RevenueViking focuses on the first step: answer or capture the call, ask useful questions, and preserve the service request for the team.

RevenueViking is based in Connecticut and is starting with practical, direct service for home-service businesses in Connecticut and nearby Northeast markets.

We are deliberately starting small. The core offer is AI receptionist configuration and structured lead capture. Calendar, CRM, SMS and other integrations are treated as separate projects when a customer actually needs them.

That keeps the service easier to explain, easier to test, and easier to deliver honestly.

What we promise

A business-specific call-intake configuration, structured caller information, urgency rules, testing and transparent scope.

What we do not promise

Guaranteed revenue, unlimited usage, automatic dispatch, enterprise SLAs or integrations that have not been scoped and tested.

Who we are building for

HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, garage-door, pest-control, cleaning and other local service businesses where phone intake matters.
