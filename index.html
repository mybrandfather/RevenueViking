Why We Built This

The Missed Call That Started Everything

RevenueViking started with a family HVAC business and a simple, painful observation: the phone would ring while everyone was out on jobs — and ring, and ring. Some weeks, dozens of calls went unanswered. Each one was a real person with a broken furnace or a dead AC unit, ready to pay for help. Most never called back.

We tried the usual fixes. Voicemail collected silence — people just don't leave messages anymore. A traditional answering service cost a fortune and read from generic scripts that frustrated customers and butchered job details. Hiring a receptionist meant $3,000+ a month for coverage that still ended at 5 PM.

So we built what we wished existed: a phone answering system that actually knows the business, answers instantly at any hour, asks the right questions, and books the job on the spot — for less than a single service call per month.

Our mission is simple: make sure no local service business ever loses another job because nobody picked up the phone.

FAQ

Questions Business Owners Ask Us

How does the AI call answering actually work?
+
When a customer calls your business number, the call is forwarded to RevenueViking AI. Our AI answers in under 3 seconds using a natural, professional voice, greets the caller in your business name, and handles the conversation from there — answering questions, qualifying the lead, and booking the appointment. You receive a full call summary as soon as the call ends.
Can the AI actually book appointments into my calendar?
+
Yes — this is one of our most powerful features. The AI connects directly to your Google Calendar, iCal, or scheduling platform. It checks your real-time availability during the call and books confirmed appointments on the spot. No double bookings, no back-and-forth. The customer gets an SMS confirmation instantly.
Can the AI transfer calls to me or a team member?
+
Yes. You can set rules for when the AI should attempt to transfer a call to a live person — for example, for very high-value jobs, complex situations, or repeat customers. If the transfer is not answered, the AI smoothly continues the conversation and handles the booking itself. Warm transfer is included on Growth and Pro plans.
Is the AI available 24/7, including weekends?
+
Absolutely. RevenueViking AI answers every call, every hour, every day — 365 days a year. That includes evenings when you're at home with your family, weekends, and public holidays. This is where most of the value is: those after-hours calls that previously went to voicemail and were never returned.
Does it work with my existing phone number?
+
Yes. We use call forwarding so your existing business number stays exactly the same. Customers dial the same number they always have — nothing changes for them. You simply set up a conditional forward (or always forward) from your number to ours. We walk you through this step-by-step during onboarding.
How long does setup take?
+
Most businesses are fully live within 3–5 business days. Our team handles everything: we configure the AI with your business information, train it on your services and pricing, set up calendar integration, test the full call flow, and only go live when everything is working perfectly. You don't need to be technical at all.
