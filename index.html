The Problem

What Happens When the Phone Rings and Everyone Is Busy?

For a local service business, the phone often rings at exactly the wrong time. Voicemail can work, but it may leave your team without the details needed to understand the job or decide how urgent it is.

RevenueViking gives callers a guided conversation instead: who they are, where service is needed, what happened, how to reach them, and whether approved emergency indicators are present.

Questions

Straight Answers Before You Book

Does the base package book appointments automatically?
+
No. The base offer focuses on receptionist setup and structured lead capture. Calendar booking can be scoped separately after your scheduling rules and software are reviewed and tested.
Does it include CRM or SMS integration?
+
Not by default. Those integrations can be valuable, but provider costs, consent requirements, field mapping and error handling need to be understood first.
Can it identify urgent requests?
+
Yes, the receptionist can be configured around the emergency indicators and escalation language your business approves. It does not replace emergency services or a qualified technician’s judgment.
How long does setup take?
+
It depends on phone routing, your call script, FAQs, urgency rules and testing. We give you a realistic timeline after reviewing the workflow rather than promising the same launch date to every business.
