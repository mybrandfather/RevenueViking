How We Keep It Honest

Capture First. Promise Less. Test Everything.

No invented diagnosis

The agent collects what the caller reports. Technical diagnosis and service decisions stay with qualified people.

No fake dispatch promise

Unless a scheduling or dispatch workflow has actually been built and tested, the AI captures the request for your team to review.

Your urgency rules

You approve the emergency indicators and escalation wording. The AI does not replace emergency services or professional judgment.

Garage Door FAQ

Common Questions

Can RevenueViking automatically book every garage door job?
+
Not as part of the base package. Scheduling rules can be complex, so calendar booking is treated as an optional integration and only promised after the workflow is scoped and tested.
Can it handle urgent requests?
+
It can identify the urgency indicators you approve and collect the relevant details. It does not replace emergency services or a qualified professional’s judgment.
Will it work during overflow or after hours?
+
That is one of the strongest starting use cases. The exact call routing depends on your existing phone setup and is confirmed during implementation.
