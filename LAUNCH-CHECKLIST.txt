PART 3 — CONNECTING FORMS TO A REAL BACKEND
=====================================================

The fastest no-code option for launch:
  1. Create a free Formspree (or Web3Forms) account
  2. Replace the JS submit simulation in js/app.js with a fetch() POST
     to your Formspree endpoint
  3. Keep the redirect to thank-you.html on success

Supabase option (if you want a lead database):
  - Create a "leads" table: id, name, business_name, email, phone,
    website, message, industry, source_page, created_at
  - Add a public insert policy (RLS) limited to the leads table
  - POST form data to the Supabase REST endpoint with your anon key
  - Pipe new rows to email via a Supabase Edge Function or Zapier

The form HTML already includes all the right field names and a honeypot,
so wiring a backend is a small change in one place (handleFormSubmit).
