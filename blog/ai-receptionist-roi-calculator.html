AI Receptionist ROI Calculator: Estimate the Opportunity With Your Own Numbers

ROI calculators are only useful when the assumptions are visible. RevenueViking’s calculator uses your own missed-call volume, lead-to-customer conversion rate and average job value to estimate how much potential work those missed opportunities represent.

The formula

Missed calls × your conversion rate × average job value = estimated revenue represented by missed opportunities. This is not the same as guaranteed recoverable revenue.

Use conservative inputs

If your close rate varies, use the lower end. If job values vary widely, use a median or conservative average. Exclude spam, wrong numbers and calls outside your service area when possible.

Add the cost side

Any ROI decision should include setup cost, monthly service cost, voice usage and any integration fees. Compare the total cost with the actual incremental jobs produced after implementation.

Measure after launch

Create a baseline before using a receptionist solution. After launch, track completed intakes, qualified leads, bookings, completed jobs and revenue. That is how you determine actual ROI.

When the math does not justify it

A very low call volume or very low customer value may not support a paid AI receptionist. In that case, improve voicemail, forms and callback discipline first.
